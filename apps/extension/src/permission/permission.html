Enable microphone access

This grants the AI Interview Co-Pilot extension permission to use your microphone so it can transcribe your side of the conversation (not just the other person).

Requesting microphone…
  1. If Chrome shows a permission prompt, click Allow.
  2. If it was blocked before, click the mic/camera icon in the address bar above, choose Allow, then press Retry.
  3. Once it says enabled, close this tab and click Start interview in the side panel.
